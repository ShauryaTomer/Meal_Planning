# High-Protein Bread Meal Categories

- Bread base: 2 slices (54g) = 133 kcal, 8.4g protein, 25.1g carbs, 4.7g fibre, 243mg sodium. User label photo.
- Protein rule: chicken only for meat. Cottage cheese unavailable.

## Categories

1. Chicken sandwiches / subs
   - Grilled chicken, chicken salad, BBQ pulled chicken, banh mi, chicken parm.
   - Add 120-150g cooked chicken. Approx 45-55g protein with bread.
   - Source: https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/photos/chicken-sandwich-recipes

2. Toasties / grilled sandwiches
   - Chicken + cheese + spinach, chicken pesto mozzarella, buffalo chicken, pizza toastie.
   - 2 slices + 120g chicken + cheese. Approx 50g protein.
   - Source: https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/tomato-mozzarella-toastie

3. Open-faced protein toast / tartines
   - Chicken avocado, chicken egg, chicken hummus, chicken Greek-yogurt salad.
   - One or two slices. Faster, easier to control calories.
   - Source inspiration: https://www.foodnetwork.com/how-to/packages/food-network-essentials/leftover-chicken-recipes

4. Chicken bread pizza
   - Bread, pizza sauce, chicken, mozzarella, vegetables. Oven or air fryer.
   - 2 slices + 120g chicken + mozzarella. Approx 48-55g protein.
   - Source: https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/user/5499001/recipe/french-bread-pizza

5. Breakfast egg toast / savoury French toast
   - Eggs soaked into bread; chicken, cheese, spinach or vegetables on top.
   - 2 slices + 2 eggs + 100g chicken. Approx 50g protein.

6. Baked egg-and-bread casserole / strata
   - Cubed bread, egg, chicken, cheese, vegetables. Batch prep; slice portions.
   - Good for breakfast meal prep. Use chicken sausage-style seasoning if wanted.

7. Chicken melt / chicken parm garlic bread
   - Bread + tomato sauce + chicken + mozzarella/parmesan. Toast or bake.
   - Source: https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/food-network-kitchen/chicken-parm-pull-apart-sandwiches-11456537

8. Crouton-style chicken salad bowls
   - Toasted bread cubes, chicken, greens, vegetables, Greek-yogurt dressing.
   - Bread becomes crunch; chicken remains main protein.

## Best First Tests

- Chicken pesto mozzarella toastie: fast.
- Chicken egg French toast: breakfast.
- Chicken bread pizza: easy custom meal.
- BBQ pulled-chicken sandwich: meal prep.

## Effort × Prep-Leverage Map

Question: How much work does recipe take from raw ingredients? How much of that work can Sunday prep remove before weekday assembly?

| | Low prep leverage | High prep leverage |
|---|---|---|
| Low effort | Open protein toast; crouton chicken salad | Toasties; chicken bread pizza; chicken egg French toast |
| High effort | Fresh fried chicken sandwich | BBQ pulled-chicken sandwich; chicken parm melt; chicken Philly; banh mi; shawarma/tikka chicken toastie |

- High effort / high prep leverage = target category. Sunday: cook chicken/filling, make sauce, chop or pickle vegetables, portion cheese. Weekday: toast/reheat/assemble in 5-10 minutes.
- High effort / low prep leverage: frying, fresh-crisp components, or sauces that do not keep well. Better as occasional cook-now meals.

### Practical Prep Packs

- Sandwich pack: cooked sliced/shredded chicken, washed greens, sliced vegetables, sauce. Bread stays separate.
- Toastie/pizza pack: cooked chicken, grated cheese, chopped vegetables, portioned sauce. Assemble from cold.
- French-toast pack: cooked chicken and vegetables. Whisk egg fresh; cook 5-8 minutes.
- BBQ chicken: batch-cook shredded chicken plus sauce. Freeze individual portions; toast bread fresh.

### High-Effort, High-Prep Recipes To Collect Next

1. BBQ pulled-chicken sandwich: slow-cook then shred/sauce; freeze portions.
2. Chicken parm melt: bread/cook cutlets, make tomato sauce, portion mozzarella; later reheat and toast.
3. Chicken Philly: cook chicken, peppers, onions, cheese sauce; later reheat filling and toast bread.
4. Chicken banh mi: marinate/cook chicken, pickle vegetables, make spicy mayo; later assemble.
5. Chicken shawarma or tikka toastie: marinate/cook chicken, make yogurt sauce, prep vegetables; later press in bread.

## Notes

- Bread already adds useful protein. Main protein still needs chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, cheese, or beans.
- Sodium starts at 243mg per two slices. Go lighter on sauces, deli meat, cheese if sodium matters.
